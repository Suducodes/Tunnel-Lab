    The model behind every number

    Resonant tunneling diode

    The double-barrier potential, tilted by the bias, is cut into 0.25 nm slices. A transfer matrix gives the exact transmission T(E) of each energy. Current is the Tsu–Esaki integral:

    J = (q m* kT / 2π²ħ³) ∫ T(E) ln[(1+e^{(E_F−E)/kT}) / (1+e^{(E_F−qV−E)/kT})] dE

    Emitter Fermi energy from 10¹⁸ cm⁻³ doping. Constant effective mass, no space charge.

    Single-electron transistor

    Orthodox theory. The island charge N changes by single tunnel events with rate

    Γ = ΔF / (q² R) / (1 − e^{−ΔF/kT})

    Charging energy E_c = e²/CΣ from a disk island (CΣ = 8ε r), with Cg : Cs : Cd = 0.30 : 0.35 : 0.35. The master equation is solved for the stationary probabilities of N.

    Quantum dot

    Constant-interaction model: adding electron N+1 into orbital k costs

    μ(N+1) = E_c(N + ½) − e(CgVg + CsVs + CdVd)/CΣ + ε_k

    Spin-degenerate orbitals spaced Δ = 2πħ²/m*A. Sequential tunnelling with fast internal relaxation, tunnel rate from the WKB barrier factor.

    Teaching model. Left out: self-consistent charge (Schrödinger–Poisson), NEGF, cotunnelling, spin and phonon effects. Numbers are representative of real devices but not fitted to any one. The 3D electrons are a kinetic Monte-Carlo of the same rates, slowed from GHz to human time.
